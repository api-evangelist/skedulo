# Skedulo

> Skedulo is the Deskless Productivity Cloud: a field service and mobile workforce management platform for scheduling, dispatching, optimizing, and tracking deskless workers. Its API surface spans 21 REST services plus a full GraphQL API, webhooks/triggered actions, a CLI, SDKs, and an MCP server.
